# Knowledge base changelog

## 2026-10-04 — first update pass
- New source: growth.design "C.L.E.A.R. Design Masterclass: The Psychology of UI Design" (course, Sep 2026). C.L.E.A.R. (Copywriting, Layout, Emphasis, Accessibility, Reward) noted as a concept; below the 3-source threshold for its own entry.
- Re-reviewed the 8 weakest principles: Cashless Effect, Group Attractiveness Effect, Backfire Effect, Nudge, Decoy Effect, Cognitive Dissonance, Miller's Law, Singularity Effect.
- Grade changes (Contested → Moderate):
  - Cashless Effect — Schomburgk, Belli & Hoffmann 2024 meta-analysis (392 effect sizes, 71 papers): small but significant, shrinking over time.
  - Group Attractiveness Effect — independent replications (Carragher et al. 2019/2020, Hsieh et al. 2021, Jones 2024): tiny (~2%), memory-based.
  - Nudge — DellaVigna & Linos 2022 (126 nudge-unit RCTs, +1.4 pp) and Milkman 2021 megastudies: small, real, often short-lived.
  - Decoy Effect — Hasan et al. 2025 registered report and Wu & Cosguner 2020 field data: works in numeric side-by-side comparisons.
  - Singularity Effect — broad compassion-fade pattern supported by meta-analyses; the narrow single-victim interaction stays Contested.
- Unchanged but better sourced: Backfire Effect, Cognitive Dissonance (both Contested). Miller's Law tagline corrected to "~4 chunks, not 7" (still Contested as commonly stated).
- Not retrievable: 6 growth.design case studies now redirect to a paid course; 3 episode transcripts rate-limited.
